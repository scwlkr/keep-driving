# Hybrid Proof Region Notes

## Current decision

Keep the authored-macrograph plus disposable expedition-overlay seam for the next isolated prototype iteration. Do not graduate it into production Modules yet.

## Why

- The county topology remains one learnable authored fact across overlay seeds.
- Expedition variation is explicit, deterministic, and scoped to stable sockets/templates.
- Failed validation emits no partial topology or overlay artifact.
- The same exact bake hashes and Stable IDs produce the same visible static projection; hash mismatches and unresolved projected graph/socket references leave no partial world or vehicle. Full source invariants remain owned by the bake contract.
- The scene reuses the tracked fixed-step vehicle/camera through their public seams; it does not copy or modify the frozen Handling Truth Slice implementation.
- The seam is small enough to replace if visible play exposes weak routes or excessive authoring cost.

## Still unproven

- Keyboard completion of the two routes and shortcut at the intended proof-region scale.
- Authored crossing/event behavior beyond the current midpoint/marker projection convention.
- Bounded cell streaming, blank-terrain prevention, Streaming Drag, and sustained rendered performance.
- Human route memory, route-choice meaning, readability, inhabited feel, and decision cadence.
- Production `ContentModule`, canonical `ExpeditionManifest`, save, or migration compatibility.

The next slice should divide this projection into real bounded cells, add the first crossing/event behavior contract, and prove attach limits plus no-blank-terrain/Streaming-Drag behavior before any human route-memory session. Keep the shipping main scene and frozen Handling Truth Slice evidence untouched.
