# Decide Product Identity And Launch Scope

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

What single-player fantasy, target audience, design pillars, world promise, session loops, player journey, ethical business model, and Required / Valuable / Post-1.0 scope produce the strongest realistic Keep Driving 1.0.0?

## Answer

Resolve the title-independent product as a **premium, offline-first, single-player top-down survival-driving roguelite where every road is a build decision**.

> Build the car. Read the county. Take the risk. Bring it home.

### Product contract

- **Fantasy:** turn a wreck into a route-solving machine, restore a broken county, gamble on one more detour, and bring the car and unsecured haul home.
- **Audience:** players who want accessible Arcade-Real driving plus route planning, buildcraft, exploration, and extraction risk in 15-25 minute offline sessions; not simulation racers, party-management road-trip RPG players, idle/F2P players, or multiplayer-first players.
- **Pillars:** road choice is the game; the car is the character; return makes risk meaningful; consequences are readable and tactile at speed; crafted places support systemic trips.
- **World promise:** one connected learnable county with diegetically restored access, authored routes/landmarks/safehouses, build-conditional shortcuts, and deterministic conditions. Roads are advantages, never rails. Zombies are one encounter set, not the headline identity.
- **Roguelite contract:** expedition conditions reset and unsecured haul is at risk; the restored county, discoveries, chassis, parts, and campaign profile never wipe. If retained by D020, Expedition Mode unlocks only after primary completion.
- **Session loop:** choose an objective and loadout; read and commit through consequential route decisions; complete, detour, push, extract, or fail; then make one meaningful Garage decision. The target is a 15-25 minute expedition with interruption-safe save boundaries.
- **Journey:** first-run accessibility setup; Utility wagon ownership; controllable Garage tutorial; first safehouse expedition; first secured return and installed choice-bearing part; county restoration/build specialization; final cross-county mastery route; and, if retained by D020, post-completion Expedition Mode.
- **Business:** one paid 1.0 app containing the complete base game; no launch IAP, ads, consumables, subscription, account, mandatory network, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Consider only a substantial non-consumable expansion after the base game is complete and stable.
- **Public name:** internal work may retain the repository title, but release planning assumes a separately cleared distinctive name unless written legal and positioning review affirmatively clears the exact title.

### Scope decision

The identity, complete campaign/ending, route/build diversity, production handling/presentation, accessibility, save integrity, supported-device proof, and App Store readiness are Required.

The current provisional target retains D015's three-region / 15-landmark / six-safehouse / 15-encounter / three-chassis / six-slot / 36-part counts and 8-12-hour primary arc, while D020 sharpens completion/mastery to a bounded 20-30-hour range. Ticket 03/D026 supersedes the old fungible 24-objective presentation with independent floors: six Restoration Objectives, four Contract Definitions, six Delivery Contract Instances, six Recovery Contract Instances, and six Mastery Overlays; surplus cannot substitute across axes. These are **provisional production targets**, not already-proven minimums. Before content multiplication, Phase 3 publishes `docs/production/SCOPE_LOCK.md`: dated ship window, person-hours by discipline, cash/contractor/device/tester budgets, measured repeatable tranche, separately costed unique and mandatory integration/QA/accessibility/localization/legal/release work, then a 30% unallocated risk reserve. Every axis, discipline, budget, and critical-path date must pass. Low-confidence unique work is sampled rather than treated as zero.

If the target fails, D020 applies a ranked Required cut ladder down to the coherent fallback: two full region identities plus a compact finale, eight landmarks/three safehouses, three activity families, independent floors of three Restoration Objectives, two Contract Definitions, three Delivery Contract Instances, three Recovery Contract Instances, three Mastery Overlays, and eight Encounter Templates, plus two chassis/five slots/24 parts/four viable builds and a 5-8-hour ending with 10-15 hours of no-filler completion/mastery. The complete arc, regional contrast, route/build/return loop, decision density, accessibility, reliability, supported-device quality, and App Store readiness cannot be cut. If the fallback also fails, production scale-up stops. Do not preemptively shrink to today's prototype; do not ship filler to preserve a number.

Valuable work includes chassis/parts beyond the D020-locked Required set, expanded cosmetics/fine tuning, photo/replay/run card, Game Center/cloud, tilt, featured seeds, native iPad, extra localization, and additional mastery/encounter quantity. Post-1.0 includes new regions/arcs/chassis/parts, substantial paid expansion, larger Expedition rules, asynchronous services, multiplayer, and mods.
