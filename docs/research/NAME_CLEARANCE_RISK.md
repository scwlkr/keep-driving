# Name-clearance risk: `Keep Driving`

Status: working-title release blocker
Source check: 2026-07-11

## Confirmed primary-source facts

- An existing commercial game uses the exact title **Keep Driving**. Its official YCJY page describes it as “A ROAD TRIP MANAGEMENT RPG” and identifies YCJY Games AB in the page copyright ([official YCJY game page](https://www.whycjwhy.com/keepdriving/index.html)).
- The official Steam listing names **YCJY Games** as developer, **YCJY Games** and **Kepler Ghost** as publishers, and **February 6, 2025** as the release date ([official Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2756920/Keep_Driving/)).
- Steam describes that product as an open-road management RPG in which the player maps a cross-country route, explores a procedurally generated pixel-art world, and upgrades, customizes, and repairs a car ([official Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2756920/Keep_Driving/)).
- The Steam footer states: “YCJY games AB, the YCJY games AB logo, Keep Driving, and the Keep Driving logo are trademarks and/or registered trademarks of YCJY games AB” ([official Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2756920/Keep_Driving/)). This note records that assertion without independently determining registration status, scope, priority, or enforceability.

## Factual product and premise overlap

This repository currently uses the same internal title for a premium, offline-first, top-down survival-driving roguelite centered on route choice, an explorable road network, car upgrades, and repeated expeditions ([project product vision](../PRODUCT_VISION.md), [project game design](../GAME_DESIGN.md)). The products therefore share the exact title and several high-level product facts: driving/road-trip framing, route or map choice, world exploration, a customizable/upgradable car, and replay through changing journeys or conditions.

They also have material factual differences. The Steam product presents an early-2000s, pixel-art management RPG with hitchhikers, odd jobs, and turn-based challenge resolution. This project targets real-time top-down vehicle handling, car-only survival combat, a hostile county, safehouse restoration, and zombie pressure. Those differences are recorded for product positioning only; they are not a legal risk determination.

## No legal conclusion

The exact-title collision, adjacent game category, and premise overlap require formal clearance. This note does **not** conclude infringement, non-infringement, registrability, ownership, likelihood of confusion, or whether the title may legally be used. Store, domain, company-name, or repository-name availability alone would not answer those questions.

## Mandatory clearance-or-rename gate

`Keep Driving` remains an internal working title and public execution assumes a rename. Neutral prototype identifiers may continue, but before the Phase-2 signed iOS feasibility build, any production App ID is registered, any App Store Connect record is created, any logo/icon/store asset is commissioned or finalized, any marketing begins, or any public product name is locked, one—and only one—of these routes must reach **PASS**:

1. **Exact-title exception:** qualified trademark counsel delivers a dated written clearance for `Keep Driving`, covering the intended game/software goods and services, launch territories, storefronts, publisher identity, and relevant confusingly similar marks. Every condition in that advice, including any required written consent or license, is completed and documented. A separate documented positioning/search-confusion review must also affirm that the exact title is acceptably distinctive in the intended storefront context.
2. **Rename route:** a distinctive replacement title is selected and receives the same dated, written, qualified clearance for the same scope. A documented positioning review must also pass pronunciation, recall, storefront/domain search ambiguity, competitor confusion, and fit with the route/build/restoration promise. A rename by spelling, punctuation, subtitle, or logo styling does not pass unless counsel clears that exact replacement and the positioning review passes.

The gate is **PASS** only when the written clearance record, covered scope, cleared exact title, positioning-review evidence, date, and owner approval are archived and referenced from `docs/DECISIONS.md`. If counsel does not give affirmative clearance, limits it beyond the intended launch, requires an unmet condition, or the positioning review fails, the gate is **FAIL**. Failure of the exact-title exception sends the project to the rename route; failure of a replacement candidate requires another replacement. Internal opinion, a basic web/store/domain search, an automated trademark search, or absence of an App Store title collision cannot pass the gate.
